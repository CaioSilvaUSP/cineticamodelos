Hey, my name is Caio Silva. I am a masters student in Chemical Engineering at the University of São Paulo in Lorena, where my research focuses on process modeling using artificial neural networks. My work investigates the esterification reaction between free fatty acids and alcohols, integrating experimental kinetic and equilibrium data with data driven models. The objective is to predict reaction performance and understand the influence of key variables such as temperature, catalyst content, and molar ratio, supporting process optimization and decision making in the development of sustainable chemical processes.
